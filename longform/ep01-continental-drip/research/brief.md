**Untaken angle: "A 400-year-old clue, a joke theory, and a dead explorer."** The story has never been told as one story:
- **1620:** Francis Bacon writes that the New and Old Worlds are "broad and expanded toward the north, and narrow and pointed toward the south." I verified this in the Devey translation, *Novum Organum* Book II, Aphorism XXVII.
- **1875:** a theory that Earth was collapsing into a tetrahedron explained every one of these oddities, and it was wrong.
- **1930:** Wegener, who was right, dies on the Greenland ice cap. Greenland is one of the drip's own tips.
- **The twist:** the man who wrote the 1976 parody "Continental Drip", John C. Holden, had co-authored the real reconstruction of Pangaea breaking apart (Dietz & Holden 1970). His joke even cited the real answer.

**Why it beats what exists:**

  1. The big drip is a scar left by the breakup of Gondwana.
  2. The north-heavy land comes from the continents drifting north.
  3. The small drips are partly our brain finding patterns.

## 6. Risks and mitigations

- **A soft "why" disappoints viewers who clicked for an answer.** Give partial answers at S1, S5 and S7, and frame the honest S8 as the brand's trust promise. Never claim a single cause.
- **Comment pile-on ("Australia points north!").** Name the counterexamples ourselves first, in S8.
- **Numbers that depend on resolution.** Use ranges ("under 5%", "about two-thirds") and show the method on screen.
- **Overclaiming the future.** Label every future map as a model, and give Farnsworth as "one study."
- **Saturated plate-tectonics content** (Atlas Pro 7.2M on Pangaea). Keep the plate history at about 3 minutes and always tie it to the drip question.
- **First-mover race with Vsauce or Hank Green.** Ship within 3 weeks. Re-run the gap check the day before upload. Cut 2 Shorts (antipode overlay, Pacific drop) as funnels.
- **Monotony from too much globe.** Rotate globe, archival, data, cross-section and time-scrub. Hold no shot longer than 12 s without an event. Chapter cards every 2–4 min.
