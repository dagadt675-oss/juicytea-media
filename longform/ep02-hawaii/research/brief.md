## 3. The unclaimed angle vs the incumbents

**What exists:**
- Exact premise, both low effort: Bright Side Dark Secrets' "What Hawaii Looks Like Without Water (The 30,000 ft Drop)" (9.0K, 105 min, a sleep doc that owns those title words) and a Korean channel's "If You Drain the Hawaiian Ocean, a Mountain Taller Than Everest Appears" (2.3K, 8 days, 36 min).
- GeoPanda's 3-minute bathymetry clip (54K).
- Pacific-wide drains that probably show Mauna Kea as one segment: Unseen Works (117K, 4 days) and Origins of Everyday Things (171K, 7 months).
- Hawaii and mountain explainers that are not drains: Signore Galilei's "Hawaii's Forgotten Islands" 3.04M, Be Smart's tallest mountain 3.64M, Atlas Pro's biogeography 1.46M, GeologyHub's Nu'uanu slide 194K.
- Shorts have spent "taller than Everest" (17M) and "hidden islands" (14M).
- Midway wrecks are well covered as WWII content (House of History 3.18M, Nat Geo 2.47M), never as geology.

**What nobody has done, and we will:**
1. **A Hawaii-only drain on real bathymetry** (UH SOEST 50 m multibeam plus ETOPO), told as a story with one reveal per step.
2. **The true-height climax.** The islands are so heavy they bent the ocean floor and the crust down by as much as 8 km. Measured from that dented base, Mauna Loa stands about 17,170 m, or **56,000 ft**, almost twice Everest (USGS). Only a 38K and a 1.4K video come near it.
3. **The conveyor as a time machine.** Every island to the northwest is the Big Island's future: Maui, Kaua'i, a sliver of rock that people once farmed (Nihoa), a lone pinnacle, a coral ring (Midway), and then nothing (Kure's Darwin Point).
4. **Midway as "a battle over a drowned volcano"**, with honest wreck geography: the carriers lie 170-190 miles out on the abyssal plain, not on the atoll.
5. **The newborn.** Kama'ehuakanaloa, 3,200 ft down, is the next island and is already erupting.

Our edge over the drain clones is a story arc, not a list of depths. We pair the photoreal craft ep01 was praised for with strict honesty: no mega-tsunami hype and no fake wreck positions.

## 8. Visual plan

**The hero shots the engine must nail**, in priority order:
1. **Thumbnail-in-motion and the refill** (0:00 / 11:45). These are the same camera, and they make or break CTR and the promise. Water foam rim, turquoise depth gradient, warm raking light on basalt, VE 1.0.
2. **Payoff cut-away** (11:10). The terrain slices cleanly. The crust and Moho sag is drawn from the USGS flexure description: a depression up to 8 km under Mauna Loa, so the curve shape is plausible but schematic. The height counter climbs. The Everest mesh shares the base line and scale. This is the one shot that has to be "remarkably vivid".
3. **The Big Island standing up, with Everest** (3:30-4:30). A low camera so it reads as huge without exaggeration.
4. **Maui Nui at −400 ft** (0:14). Three islands fuse, Kaho'olawe dims, Penguin Bank surfaces.
5. **The Nu'uanu debris field with the Manhattan overlay** (2:20). Low sun so the blocks cast shadows; hypsometric tint so the drained floor is not grey mush.
6. **The time-machine flight** (6:05-7:00). Eased point-to-point moves of 1.5-3 s between islands, never a slow drift.
7. **Midway cut-away and the wreck line** (8:15 / 9:30). Use inset zooms for tiny objects: a ship is 250 m on a 300 km frame.
8. **Kama'ehuakanaloa** (10:15). A young dark cone on the drained south flank.
9. **The 1975 coast drop** (0:47). Close DEM shot, palms, water flooding in.

**Data:**
- **Main Hawaiian Islands:** UH SOEST HMRG multibeam synthesis (50 m, credit in description), blended into NOAA ETOPO 2022 15" (public domain).
- **Coastal close shots:** NCEI Hawaii coastal DEMs and USGS 3DEP 10 m.
- **The northwest chain and the Emperor seamounts:** GEBCO 2024 (attribution in description).
- **Everest:** SRTM 1".
- **Drape:** Landsat 8/9 and NASA GIBS true colour (check NAIP coverage for Hawaii).
- **Characters and props:** CC0 Quaternius/Kenney characters (the tourist, climber, seal, albatross, palms).
- **Wreck silhouettes:** home-built.
- **[PD] photos:** USGS HVO, NOAA, USFWS and NHHC; check each credit.

**One consistent look (V1):**
- Photoreal terrain everywhere, with one colour grade: warm low sun, turquoise water with a depth gradient, a pale-blue haze sky (never navy), light vignette and grain.
- **One font:** Barlow Condensed 800 for labels and counters.
- **One label style:** white text in a black pill.
- **One accent:** signal yellow (#FFD23F). It is used only for the SEA LEVEL line, the current subject's rim or highlight, and one key word. Everything around the subject is dimmed and desaturated (V5).
- **Vertical exaggeration** is 1.0 in every shot that compares heights (Everest, 33,500, 56,000). Texture shots may go up to 1.5. Never mix values in one shot.
- **Characters** appear only at close range on the real DEM with matched sun direction and shadows (V2). There is no separate stylised set anywhere in the film.

**Phone-readable rules (C1-C5, V5-V6):**
- **Pace:** a hard cut or new visual event every 3-5 s, average shot length 5 s or less, and never more than 6 s without a new element.
- **Text sizes at 1080p:** labels 64 px or more, key words 90-120 px, and big counters at least a quarter of the frame tall (about 270 px or more). Lines and arcs that matter are 8-10 px or thicker (the wreck line, slide outlines, the SEA LEVEL line).
- **Label placement:** at most 3 labels at once (aim for 1). No text in the bottom 20% (captions) or the top 8%.
- **Subject size:** whatever the narration names fills at least a third of the frame width.
- **Scale and moves:** regional altitudes. Globe or whole-chain shots last 3 s or less and take no more than 10% of runtime. Camera moves are 1.5-3 s with easing; slow drifts only under a changing overlay.
- **Focus:** one focal point per moment; one thing moves meaningfully at a time.
- **Depth gauge:** a slim vertical bar on the left edge with a moving water marker and the SEA LEVEL tick, with no running digits. Big digits appear only at the 4 big-number moments.
- **QC gate before render sign-off:** re-run the phone-sim sheet (`engine_scout/proto_phone_sim.jpg` method) on 20 frames. Any label under 60 px or any subject under a third of the frame width is a fail.
