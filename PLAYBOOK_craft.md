# Myth Juice playbook, part 2: craft

Sections 7-13 of the playbook (pacing, visuals, text, humour, audio, ending, packaging). Read PLAYBOOK.md first: its Read this first list, owner rules, tags, source keys and precedence apply here. All px sizes are at 1080p.

## 7. Pacing

### DO (proven)

- Cut or add a new visual event every 3-5 s (every 2-3 s in minute 1), average shot 4-4.5 s or less; change shot scale every 5-7 s, alternate the subject between the left and right thirds, and fly point to point in 1.5-3 s with easing. (ep01's problem was sameness of scale and framing, not cut count: vu measured 5.3 s between detected cuts, on benchmark, but a longest uncut stretch of 32.2 s at 5 fps; Contour Atlas 3.5-4.2 s per shot; vu diag.audit finding 11; hunt lessons C1, C2) [measured]
- Pause unevenly: 0.2-0.5 s inside a chapter, about 0.4 s after a turn line, 0.7-1.1 s before key reveals, 1.5-1.8 s after big beats, and a 0.8-0.9 s voice gap at each chapter card; the card stays up longer (section 8) and narration resumes under the end of its hold and its dissolve-out. (Lake Peigneur gaps of 1.06-1.50 s left holes at all 6 cards, bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/QC_lake_peigneur_v4_full_2026-10-28.md open item 4; R6/voice_audio) [measured]

### DONT (banned)

- Never hold one composition longer than 7 s (10 s for a live change, meaning a drain, flood, ice peel or time slider whose state and camera keep moving), a frozen frame for 1.5 s or more (freezedetect -45 dB), or a near-static run over 2 s (mean frame difference under 0.15, the still_s measure of retention_pack.py). Gate with one detector pair: qc_video.py max_s_between_cuts (every stretch over 7 s is checked by eye, and only a live change may reach 10 s) plus still_s. Do not cite qc_video's 10.99 s mean between cuts as proof of too few cuts: vu's detector found 5.3 s on the same film. (ep01 v3 max_s_between_cuts 84.8 s, ep01/research/qc_v3.json; 36 s of one image in Lake Peigneur, bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/QC_lake_peigneur_v4_full_2026-10-28.md sections 2, 4; look section 9; vu diag.audit finding 11) [measured]
- Never put a subscribe, like or comment ask, or a Juice Check/Subscribe graphic, inside the story; no call-to-action graphic before the last 20 s. (YouTube: the 4:57-5:07 ask broke the flow before the strongest reveal; subs +0; ctx; vu diag.audit finding 9) [reviewed]
- Never cut or blink to black mid-film, and never show more than 8 frames (0.27 s) of black except after the last frame; keep the outgoing scene drawing until the incoming fade ends. (1.43 s of black at 0:53 read as buffering, R6/synthesis fix 3; the published ep01 v3 still had 0.83 s of black at 455.27-456.10 s, ep01/research/qc_v3.json; R6/finish_ch6 S10-3; engine/ENGINE.md) [reviewed]

## 8. Visuals

### DO (proven)

- Judge every draft and every review pass at phone size (a 480x270 downscale or a 390x219 sheet), not only on a desktop frame. (ep01 AVD: mobile 2:11 (14.1%) vs TV 8:24 (54.1%), n = 43 views with about 19 on mobile, direction only; ctx) [measured]
- Build the film as one visual world: photoreal Earth stages (globe, DEM terrain and seafloor, paper cut-outs on the map) as WORLD, then one shared POST grade, then a crisp UI layer drawn after post, so labels, outlines, arrows, numbers and hero fills never pass through bloom, DOF, tonemap, vignette or grain. (YouTube criticised ep01's stylised corridor; 12 visual languages in 15 min; vu diag.audit finding 4; look section 1.1, U1; ep02/lab/globe/cineglobe.js) [reviewed]
- Make tilted full-bleed oblique and terrain shots the signature look for 60% or more of runtime. (74% of ep01 frames were at least 45% near-black, only 7.1% full-bleed; vu diag.audit finding 1; look section 3) [measured]
- Keep story shots at mean luma 70/255 or more, the film at 85-130, near-black pixels (luma under 18) at 15% or less, and no run over 4 s below luma 40; show a night-side globe only when the city lights are the point. (ep01 mean luma about 40; benchmarks 105-166; vu diag.audit finding 6; look section 2) [measured]
- Make whatever the narration names fill at least a third of the frame width (aim for 50-70% of frame height), every highlighted shape at least 25 px wide on a 390 px phone frame (about 120 px at 1080p) and every meaning-carrying icon or pin about 64 px or more; otherwise move closer or add an inset zoom. (in ep01 Greenland, Florida, Baja, Kamchatka and India were each 'a few millimetres wide on a phone', hunt lessons T8; look section 2; hunt lessons C4) [measured]
- Draw hero highlights in the antipode style: hero orange #FF5A36, one hero shape per frame, 55-66% fill, 5-6 px white outline, soft glow, surroundings dimmed and desaturated. (YouTube called the antipode overlay 'remarkably vivid and professional', ctx; look sections 1.2, 9) [reviewed]
- Use the look-book palette: water and second colour #3DE3FF, numbers, arrows and gauge #FFD23F, text #F4F6FA, red #E8412C sparingly for danger, a navy gradient #0B1D28 to #05070D for space (never flat black), a sky gradient on obliques. (look section 1.2) [reviewed]
- Prove key claims with 3D data overlays on the photoreal globe, and keep the overlay and its colour meaning on screen for the whole spoken claim; state the meaning with coloured headline words inside the one allowed text block (for example LAND in hero orange and WATER in cyan), never with a legend box. (the antipode overlay first showed for 1.2-1.5 s and was fixed to hold 3:47.7-3:53.5; R6/SUMMARY.md S3-8; R6/finish_ch2; look section 9) [reviewed]
- Show change live in one uncut camera with a slow continuous move (drain, flood, ice peel, time slider) for 4-10 s while the gauge ticks and a riser plays; the 10 s limit in section 7 covers all four kinds as long as the state and the camera keep moving. (the live drain took the panel teen from 3 to 8; vu panel drain; vu diag.bench findings 3-4) [reviewed]
- Give each film one persistent gauge for its one changing variable (sea level, depth, year) in a fixed corner at 12-15% of frame height, in the same place in every mode for the chapter, ticking while it moves. (Contour Atlas WATER LEVEL widget; vu diag.bench finding 3) [benchmark]
- Anchor every visual event to the narration word timings: highlights land 2-4 frames before their key word and nothing lands more than 0.2 s after it; a counter never passes through wrong values and a label never covers its own pin. (ep01 lags of 0.4-2.8 s; R6/SUMMARY.md; ep01/research/bench_visual.md section 3; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/pkg/COLAB_RUN.md section 7) [reviewed]
- Make every animation physically and geographically true to the story: objects follow real paths and corridors, sink and vanish where the narration says, and drained water exposes a muddy, cracked bed, never a black patch. (owner's second-by-second review of Lake Peigneur v2 found 12 such defects; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/pkg/COLAB_RUN.md section 7) [reviewed]
- Render terrain at vertical exaggeration 1.0 in every shot that compares heights and at most 2x elsewhere (2x default), never mix values within a shot, and say 'heights exaggerated' once in the narration when above 1.0. (3x looked like crumpled foil and game terrain; vu panel drain; look sections 8 B3, 9) [reviewed]
- Reuse the paleo-globe module for deep time (GPlates snapshots, a continuous front, a zipper, named-plate gags), with a highlight and a trail-laying tip marker on each moving plate, within the globe limits below. (the Gondwana breakup was 'the best animation in the film'; R6/synthesis keep; R6/panel_fan) [reviewed]
- Show public-domain archival stills full-bleed and always moving (Ken Burns 1.5-3% per 10 s) in the shared grade: at most 3 s per still, 5 s per clip and 1 per minute, never a static portrait under an aside, and back to an animated scene within 10 s. (vu diag.audit finding 3; R6/panel_casual; look section 4 S12) [reviewed]
- Join consecutive shots that share subject and framing (within about 25%) with one continuous camera move or an 8-frame dissolve (assemble_edl.py --xfade J:8), never a hard cut, and never dissolve between differently sized globes in under 0.5 s. (S1-3, S8-2, S6-3; R6/SUMMARY.md; scratch:engine/finalize_v3b.log) [reviewed]
- When a 3D character is used (in ep02 only the look book's one recurring character on the real terrain, after its motion test passes the phone QC; see the low-poly ban below), match the clip's playback speed to its root speed, check its travel direction with a foot logger, and accept the shot only when the stance foot drifts 4 px/frame or less. (7 major character defects, after fixes 0.5-3.6 px/frame; R6/SUMMARY.md; R6/fix_ch1; look U22) [measured]
- Draw chapter cards last (on top) on a near-opaque plate over a still-moving bright shot, never a navy frame: 0.26 s dissolve in, 1.1 s hold, 0.26 s dissolve out; the voice gap is 0.8-0.9 s from the card's start, and narration may continue under the end of the hold and the dissolve-out. (owner review of Lake Peigneur v2; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/pkg/COLAB_RUN.md section 7 item 1; brightness rules above) [reviewed]
- Pad equirect overlay bounds to plus or minus 181.5 degrees and fill each ring's -360/0/+360 copies into one opaque mask before tinting. (lib/globe.js drops pixels outside u (0,1), leaving a date-line hairline; ep02/lab/type/shots.js; commit 79e272e) [measured]
- Fix satellite tile seams (different Esri acquisitions) with the water-seam polish or a lake-mask blur before compositing. (bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/orig/LONGFORM_lake_peigneur_production_table.md; bundle:san_juan/BUILD_REPORT.md caveat 7) [reviewed]
- Keep any flashing at 3 flashes per second or fewer. (bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/orig/LONGFORM_lake_peigneur_production_table.md) [benchmark]
- Build every ep02 shot with the shot toolkit engine/www/ep/ep02/shared.js (API.md): one WebGLRenderer for the page, stages created lazily (only the stages active at a frame render), each stage through the same shared grade pass, UI drawn after the grade; reproduce a thumbnail as frame 1 with its plate settings, its crop as a lens shift and its masked grade ported to the shader. (frame 1 vs the label-free NO OCEAN thumbnail: SSIM 0.969 at 320x180; lab_hawaii.html renders stayed pixel-identical after the stage changes, MAE 0; research/ep02/foundation/ch1/frame1_ssim.txt) [measured]
- Recover a lost plate camera with a camera search scored on land / water / sky layout agreement, not grey SSIM: grey SSIM sat at 0.55-0.60 for every candidate, the layout score separated them (best 0.91 at 192x108). (engine/www/ep/ep02/tools/camsearch.html; CAMS.mauinui_one) [measured]

### DONT (banned)

- Never let an element appear or vanish in a single frame: enter and exit over at least 6 frames (fade, scale or wipe) unless it is a deliberate stamp with its own SFX, and never hide a moving or visible element with a visibility toggle. (18 of 97 round-6 findings were pops; R6/SUMMARY.md) [measured]
- Never use a small centred globe on black as the default shot: whole-globe-in-space shots take at most 10% of runtime in total and 4 s each, filling the frame height, with clouds at 0-0.35 and off the subject on globes that carry a fact. One exception: a proof overlay (antipode style) or a paleo sequence may hold a frame-filling globe longer while the camera keeps moving, at most 8 s per framing before the scale or side changes, and it still counts toward the 10%. These numbers replace the brief's 'globe shots 3 s or less'. (vu diag.audit finding 1; vu panel globe; look sections 2-3; the praised antipode overlay held 5.8 s, 3:47.7-3:53.5, R6/finish_ch2; the praised Gondwana sequence ran over 20 s, R6/SUMMARY.md S7) [measured]
- Never cut a stylised, differently lit set into the photoreal film: no gradient voids, low-poly characters or scenes, Kenney block kids or flat-vector interiors. In ep02 human beats use paper people (section 10); a CC0 3D character is allowed only as the one recurring character on the real terrain whose motion test passed the phone QC. Any figure, paper or 3D, stands on the real terrain at 35-40% of frame height or more, same sun, soft contact shadow; a figure that cannot be that big in the shot does not go in it. (YouTube on ep01's 7:35-7:50 corridor, ctx; vu diag.audit finding 5; look sections 9 and U22) [reviewed]
- Never open a scene on an empty or near-empty frame; no blank lead-in over 6 frames under narration. (12:55.87 empty navy frame 'looks like a missing render layer'; R6/synthesis fix 8) [reviewed]
- Never move a 3D character's root while it plays Idle, and never cut away from Roll or another acrobatic clip before it ends; play it once:true at speed = clip duration / path time, then crossfade to Idle over about 0.25 s. (S10-2, S3-4, S5-3; R6/fix_ch3) [measured]
- Never spin a globe faster than about 14 degrees per frame at its peak, and add sub-frame motion blur to any high-contrast pan over about 20 px/frame. (a 38 degrees/frame spin strobed; R6/finish_ch6 S10-6, S10-11) [measured]
- Never use one shot template more than twice in a film; a template is the same framing, motion and layout, so drain steps at different places, with different camera scales and their own reveal, count as different shots. Give every list item its own transition. (four future models in one template; R6/synthesis fix 17; R6/panel_fan) [reviewed]
- Never use grids, thumbnail mosaics, 4-way split screens, overlapping pins, ghosted texture backgrounds or colour legends; state colour meaning with coloured headline words and show lists as full-frame tilted shots joined by whip-pans (1-1.5 s per quick item, 4-7 s per explained item). (ep01's 12:00 thumbnails were 36x24 px on a phone; vu diag.audit findings 6, 10) [measured]
- Never draw a meaningful line thinner than 6 px, or a meaning-carrying icon or pin smaller than about 64 px across (the must-read text floor; highlighted shapes about 120 px, see DO): lines get a 9-12 px core with a dark under-stroke 8 px wider plus glow, arrows a 15 px body and a 50-63 px head, outlines 5-6 px, and every line draws on with a moving head dot. (ep01 strokes were 3-5 px, about 1 px on a phone; vu diag.audit finding 12; R6/synthesis fix 18) [measured]
- Never ship compositing or render artefacts: inset globes masked to their disc with a soft rim, overlays kept off characters, graphics kept inside their tiles, and no camera-sun specular smudge, additive blob sprites, dark smears as text plates, rectangle-clipped fills, texture seams or hairlines; check every overlay at full resolution. (S4-4, S5-2, S8-4, R6/SUMMARY.md; vu diag.audit finding 7) [measured]
- Never show mock documents, fake papers with grey dash 'text' or placeholder graphics; use a real public-domain scan cropped to 5 big words or fewer, or tell the moment on the map. (ep01's 'THE DRIP, REVISITED' mock paper risked a false claim; vu diag.audit finding 8) [reviewed]
- Never leave crossfade ghosts: clear the previous beat's elements before a card's plate appears, and hard-cut between contiguous diagram beats. (bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/QC_lake_peigneur_v4_full_2026-10-28.md item 7) [measured]
- Never use a stock clip without a per-file licence check on the day of use (Pexels and Pixabay are not CC0; Wikimedia varies), and never film or franchise clips. (bundle:bats/bats_not_blind_script_v2_2026-10-26.md) [reviewed]
- Never let a hold's push or heading drift accumulate through a flight: ch1's drift ran from the shot start and landed the flight 6.8 degrees off the ONE ISLAND framing; fade hold moves out during a flight and restart them on landing. (engine/www/ep/ep02/ch1.js cam) [measured]

## 9. Text and graphics

### DO (proven)

- Size text by cap height with ep02/lab/type/type.js: must-read text (place labels, name plates, gauge titles) 64 px or more (about 13 px on a phone), connectors at least 48 px, headlines in Anton at 96-120 px with at most 4 words. Since 2026-11-08 type.js FLOOR is {must: 64, any: 48}, and the ep02 toolkit (engine/www/ep/ep02/shared.js makeUI) enforces caps, one text block, 10 words, 4-word headlines and the safe area on every frame; other pages still need the hand check in section 16. (ep01 caps were 26-35 px, 5-7 px on a phone; the type prototype took the panel teen from 2 to 8; vu diag.audit finding 2; look sections 2, 9) [reviewed]
- Use one type system: Anton for headlines and numbers, Barlow Condensed 800 caps for labels, #F4F6FA text on a rectangular rgba(6,9,15,0.86) plate with an 8 px accent bar in the colour of the thing it names, and always a plate (not just a scrim) on bright terrain or sky. (look sections 1.2, 1.3, 9) [reviewed]
- Show 6-10 big vivid numbers per long-form, each landing on its spoken word. (owner rule; ctx 'The channel') [owner]
- Turn each hero number into an image on its word (a true-scale prop, a gauge move, an overlay or a counter), with no single device used more than 3 times in a film; a counter uses Anton at 150-190 px cap height (14-18% of frame height). (look section 2; R6/synthesis fix 17) [reviewed]
- Show at most one text block plus one map label plus the gauge (10 visible words or fewer); keep must-read text in the top 62% of the frame (above y=670), out of the top 8%, inside a 5% safe margin and in the third opposite the subject; rotate 3-4 layouts. (phone controls cover the bottom; vu diag.tech finding 1; vu panel type; look section 2) [reviewed]
- In a shot's ui(), draw outlines and lines first, then the text block, then map labels with the auto placer (right, left, above, below inside the safe area and clear of the block): an island outline crossed the MAUI plate and the MAUNA LOA label sat on the payoff number block until the order changed; the ep02 toolkit logs every floor, block, word-count and safe-area breach per frame as [typeqc] FAIL. (engine/www/ep/ep02/shared.js makeUI; research/ep02/foundation) [reviewed]

### DONT (banned)

- Never show sources, citations, author-year names, page numbers, data credits, 'real NASA data', 'computed from data' or 'SIMPLIFIED' lines on screen; say a simplification once in the narration and put every credit only in the description. (owner rule; ep01 'DIETZ & HOLDEN (1970)' at 11:40, vu diag.audit finding 8; older 'MSHA 1981, p.21' tags, bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/orig/LONGFORM_lake_peigneur_script_v3.md) [owner]
- Never use emojis anywhere: on screen, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, Shorts titles, pinned comments or replies. (owner rule; ctx 'The channel') [owner]
- Never use grey on-screen text, pill-shaped labels, Bebas or near-transparent plates. (ep01 grey 'NEARLY 9 IN 10 OF US' and an unreadable pill at 5:20; vu diag.audit finding 2; look section 1.3) [reviewed]
- Never drop a qualifier ('MOST OF', 'NEARLY', 'ABOUT', 'FROM ITS TRUE BASE') to fit a word cap; use a kicker of up to 2 words or show the share as a number. (type prototype overclaim, blocker B1; look section 8) [reviewed]
- Never let text overlap another graphic, label, headline or subtitle, leave its container or appear twice; every text element sits inside its panel with padding, centred on its own panel, with a dark backing on bright ground. (S6-12, S8-3, S3-7, S2-8, R6/SUMMARY.md; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/QC_lake_peigneur_v4_full_2026-10-28.md open items 2-3) [measured]
- Never show a readable caption or gag card for less than 2 s, and never bring it in after its spoken word or the joke. (the 'ACUMINATI' pill showed for 0.7 s; R6/SUMMARY.md S1-6; R6/synthesis fix 17) [reviewed]
- Never show more than one time counter per sequence or a counter over a picture that does not change with it; fade each number out before the next label fades in. (the 10:00-10:13 counter restarted, 'wait, is it repeating?'; R6/synthesis fix 10) [reviewed]
- Never stop a highlighter sweep mid-word: reveal whole words and end the marker 6 px or more past the last glyph. (3 cards cut mid-word; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/QC_lake_peigneur_v4_full_2026-10-28.md item 13; bundle:lake_peigneur_v4/pkg/CHANGES_v4.md) [measured]

## 10. Humour

### DO (proven)

- Land one dry, true punchline every 60-90 s exactly on a cut, with a 1.0-1.4 s music dip of -9 to -12 dB (mix.json 'dips', 0.25 s ramps) right before it. (ep01 gag rhythm praised by all 4 lenses; R6/panel_fatigue; ep01/audio/mix_v3_cut.json) [reviewed]
- Put a 2-6 s in-world visual gag about every 2 minutes, made with real animation on the real terrain (paper standee hop, drain plug, true-size drag that does not fit, whip-pan to the punchline); give every funny line its own visual and use comic SFX only on gags. (the Wegener hop scored 8/10 with the teen; vu panel story; vu diag.bench finding 6) [reviewed]
- Build jokes only from verified facts delivered deadpan, and keep 1-2 sourced reserve jokes for length cuts. (San Juan humour 8.0; bundle:san_juan/QC_san_juan_pig_war_2026-10-28.md) [reviewed]
- Use the JUICE CHECK stamp (FALSE / TRUE / IT'S COMPLICATED) only as an on-the-word verdict beat, about 4 per film at most, never paired with a call to action. (praised as the brand device; the stamp plus SUBSCRIBE at 4:57 was faulted; R6/synthesis; ctx) [reviewed]
- Use paper cut-outs of real public-domain photos for human beats, at most 1-1.5 min per film: clean matte (erode 2-3 px, no spill), a warm off-white 2-4 px paper edge, a fold-out base tab, a soft contact shadow, a pop-up hinge starting at 30-40 degrees, landing on the true coordinates, at 35-40% of frame height. (vu panel story; look sections 4 S10-S11, 8, 9) [reviewed]

### DONT (banned)

- Never place gags, comic SFX or music stings over deaths, disasters or war graves; treat wrecks as war graves, with no gore and no 'treasure' framing. (hunt red kill shot 5; brief sections 4, 7, 12) [reviewed]
