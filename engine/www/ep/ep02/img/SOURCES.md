# ep02 image sources (public domain only)

Every photo in `img/` and `img/paper/` is public domain: a work of the U.S. federal government (FSA/OWI, USFWS, NPS, USGS) or catalogued as such by the Library of Congress and Wikimedia Commons. Checked 2026-11-09. Credits go in the video description only, never on screen. Chapter folders (`img/chN/`) keep their own `LICENSE.md`.

The paper cut-outs (`img/paper/*.png`) were cut with `tools/paper_cut.py` from the specs in `tools/paper_specs/` (crop box, keep/drop polygons, hand tints). The matte came from the IS-Net general segmentation model (isnet-general-use, Apache-2.0, a matting tool only: downloaded to the scratchpad, used, deleted; never in git). No image was generated. Edits made to every cut-out: background removed, 1-2 px eroded edge, resized; the JS kit adds the paper edge and the film grade at load time.
