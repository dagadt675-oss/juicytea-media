# ch1 toolkit requests (2026-11-09, chapter 1 build)

Each item is already worked around locally in `ch1.js`; nothing in `shared.js` was changed.

1. **Sub-frame motion blur for whip-pans.** `blurred(shot, w0, w1, n, shutter)` in ch1.js wraps a shot's `draw` and averages n renders at sub-frame offsets (180-degree shutter, running average through `ctx.globalAlpha`, 3 renders with `draft`, `{"noblur":1}` turns it off). Used on the two whips (s013 to s014, s025 to Oʻahu), 5 frames out and 6-8 frames in. Cost: n terrain renders per blurred frame (about 10 frames in ch1). A shared version (U14) should live in `terrainShot` as `blur: [t0, t1]`.
2. **Data that a mask needs must exist before the first draw.** `terrainShot` calls `mask()` before `world()`, so a mask built lazily in `world()` is missing on a single still (the demo's cap fill had this). ch1 wraps such shots with `ready(shot, asyncFn)`. Suggest a `prepare: async () => {}` key on `terrainShot`.
3. **C1 camera keys.** `keyAt` eases to a stop at every key; long multi-key moves need one velocity curve. ch1 has `spline(keys)` (Hermite, zero velocity only at the ends and at keys marked `stop`). Worth exporting next to `flight` and `orbitPath`.
4. **Hero fill brightness follows the stage light.** Without a masked look the fill is `heroCol` lit by the sun, so under a low or side sun it reads maroon or brick red rather than #FF5A36 (Penguin Bank, the south flank; round-1 stills). Round 2 workaround: ch1 draws these fills after the grade with `projRings(view, rings, hf)` (every ring vertex projected at its own ground height, rings never cut) and `ui.fill(..., {fill: 0.55})`, so the colour is exact; the stage mask is no longer used for them. A shared `ui.fillGround(view, rings)` (or a light-independent stage fill) would let every chapter do the same.
5. **Dry seafloor at ground level is a featureless plane** (round 2: ch1 scatters seeded true-size rock and coral rubble, 0.4-2.6 m, around the ʻAuʻau gag with `drawRock`, keeping the walk lane clear; a shared ground-detail option would serve every paper gag on drained ground). The ʻAuʻau/Kalohi floor is ETOPO 15" (about 460 m); at 15-50 m camera height the paper gags stand on a smooth brown floor. `tubAmt` patches show as a grid of squares from 300-1,500 m (value-noise lattice), so ch1 leaves them off. A close-range ground texture (sand, reef rock, scattered boulders) for paper beats would help s008 and every later paper gag on drained ground.
6. **No vegetation or camp objects in the stage.** The Halapē coconut grove (64 palms at true positions and heights from the 10 m grid), the tents and the dust are drawn 2D in `world()` after the grade and tinted per light state. Instanced 3D palms in the stage's own lighting would be better at close range.
7. **No stars in the stage sky.** ch1 draws a seeded star field only where `view.ground()` finds sky.
8. **Region hint (data owner):** the halape grid is today's post-1975 ground, so ch1 renders the pre-1975 coast as the sea 3.5 m lower against it and returns to 0 m on {drop}. A `film.pre1975: {level: -3.5}` hint in regions.json would document that for other users (s044-s046, s086-s088).
9. **Photo licence pointer.** `img/ch1/halape_1975.jpg` (USGS, public domain) is logged in `img/ch1/LICENSE.md`; a pointer line in `img/SOURCES.md` / `engine/ASSETS.md` is the orchestrator's (ch1 may not edit them).
10. **Round 2 notes (2026-11-09).** (a) The clone gate counts the lit dry reef flat as tan under the 'warm'/'gold' looks (s007-s008 at 17 % tan, drain-clone distance 9.3-10.3). ch1 leans the reef albedo cool (`reefCol` about [0.06, 0.066, 0.08]) and uses the 'day' look for the ride: tan 1 %, distance 11.4-11.9, gate PASS. A preset for "dry reef flat in daylight" that passes the gate would help ch3/ch4 drains. (b) `engine/www/ep/ep02/API.md` section 6 "Known data faults" is out of date after the region round 3 (laperouse, lalo_2018_before/after, film hints); ch1's Oʻahu landing already uses `pali.film.today` (sun [75, 55], foamAmt 0.25). (c) A low sun ahead of the camera puts a specular smudge on dry or wet ground (s007 at sun azimuth 118 while looking ESE); ch1 keeps the sun at least 55 degrees off the view direction. A `glintAmt` that also drops the seabed specular would make this safer.
11. **Round 8 notes (2026-11-09, review fixes).** (a) The stage's water is opaque after about 20 m (`aBody = 1 - exp(-depth / 7)` is hard-coded), so a slope cannot be seen continuing under the water into the blue: ch1 fakes "falls into blue depth" with a navy `waterCols` ramp that darkens away from the waterline (`wRamp` [5, 40, 60, 700]) plus a dense, low blue haze on the new sea surface (`fog` [0.3, 0.0004, 0.45]). A water-clarity uniform would let the drained-slope shots show real depth. (b) The foam rim radius (`foamPx * fwidth(depth) + foamM`) has no cap: on a steep edge seen from afar it traced a surf line 30-60 m deep (Penguin Bank after it drowned); ch1 drops `foamPx` to 0.3 during the rise. A `foamMaxM` cap would fix it for everyone. (c) Land at the 10 m / 30 m Halapē resolution has no near detail (`nearAmt` only acts on seabed, `sub`): at the framings a phone needs (the grove a third of the width) every colour texel is about 100 px wide. A seeded ground-cover overlay was tried and read as lens spots (removed); a land `nearAmt` (rock and shrub micro-albedo faded by pixel footprint) would help s016-s023 and the Pali shots. (d) Between -6 and +4 m the albedo blends seabed into the satellite colour (`smoothstep(-6, 4, h)`), so a beach the sea uncovers shows today's dark water pixels; ch1 sets the halape `reefCol` to sand, which only half helps. (e) `blurred()` now takes a per-time sample count (14 on the fastest 2-3 whip frames).
